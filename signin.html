Common questions
Do I need a brokerage account?
No. PulseStock is a standalone research platform. You get real-time Level II and Time & Sales data without opening or funding any brokerage account.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
Start Basic or Trader with full access for 7 days, no charge. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be billed a cent.
What AI model powers the analysis?
Free gets standard AI (3/day). Basic gets unlimited analysis powered by Claude Sonnet 4.6. Trader gets Claude Opus 4.7 — the most powerful model available — for institutional-quality research.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — cancel anytime from your account settings with one click. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata if cancelled early.
What is dark pool flow data?
Dark pool data shows buy vs sell dollar flow from off-exchange trading — where institutional investors trade large blocks away from public markets. It's a key signal of where smart money is positioned on a stock.
Is there an enterprise or team plan?
For team access or white-label licensing contact us at hello@pulsestock.com for custom enterprise pricing.
